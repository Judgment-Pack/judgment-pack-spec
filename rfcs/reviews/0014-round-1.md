## Findings (verbatim)

**Verdict: mergeable after fixes F1–F14 below.** Several central claims disagree with the owning repositories.

I reviewed the working-tree RFC and index against spec base `587ebc5`, gateway `origin/main` at `03582d9`, and runtime `origin/main` at `0c8a4b6`. RFC 0014 is currently untracked; its reviewed SHA-256 is `f1c92c311bf93a3f44bd7d1b4373104e7b065c01262a65592aea85e5f056e173`. Line references below identify that [RFC file](rfcs/0014-lineage-record-and-action-binding.md).

1. **F1 — MAJOR: a rehearsal with valid citations is accepted, not refused.**

   **RFC, L294–296:** “On the runtime's side the conformance is shape refusal: a citation document not of the grammar is refused on every surface before the project is read, and a rehearsal with citations is refused.”

   **Source:** ADR-0033 says a rehearsal records nothing. The CLI test explicitly supplies valid citations with `--rehearsal`, expects exit `0`, and checks that no record was appended. The MCP test likewise expects success with citations and `rehearsal: true`. [CLI test](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-runtime/blob/0c8a4b6/internal/cli/audit_test.go#L620), [MCP test](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-runtime/blob/0c8a4b6/internal/mcp/project_test.go#L2170).

   **Smallest edit:** Replace “a rehearsal with citations is refused” with “a rehearsal accepts structurally valid citations but writes no record.” Correct the same refusal claims at L99 and L214.

2. **F2 — MAJOR: citation resolution does not require the cited receipt to pass its ladder.**

   **RFC, L188–189:** “from the action to its receipts (§4 step 5): each citation resolves and the cited receipt passes the ladder under the verifier's key, or `citation-unresolved`.”

   **Source:** Step 5 compares the enumerated session name, filename stem and signature string. Whether the cited receipt verifies is its **own finding**. The implementation deliberately indexes signatures even from receipts rejected for unrelated malformed content. Thus a citation can resolve while its target reports `malformed` or `signature-mismatch`. Overall verification still fails. [Gateway SPEC §4](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/SPEC.md#L417), [implementation](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/go/verify.go#L593).

   **Smallest edit:** Say “each citation resolves by the three string comparisons, or `citation-unresolved`; the cited receipt’s verification status is reported independently.” Qualify the Summary’s “exists under the gateway’s key” as a conclusion requiring the relevant verification findings, not resolution alone. Preserve the executor’s separate requirement that cited receipts actually verify.

3. **F3 — MAJOR: the shared reference fields do not constitute identical acceptance grammars.**

   **RFC, L147–149:** “It is the gateway's grammar (§1.2a, §3a), and the runtime adopts it unchanged so that one verifier resolves every citation without a second grammar.”

   **Source:** The gateway verifier tolerates unknown members at any depth in signed receipts. Runtime ADR-0033 requires exactly three citation members; the executor also explicitly rejects a fourth member. These are different accepted input sets. Additionally, L146’s “non-negative integer” omits the canonical-domain upper bound, `2^53−1`. [Gateway SPEC §6](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/SPEC.md#L674), [executor step 6](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/docs/design/executor.md), [runtime parser](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-runtime/blob/0c8a4b6/internal/audit/audit.go#L185).

   **Smallest edit:** Replace “adopts it unchanged” with “adopts its required fields and value forms, while requiring exactly those three members on input.” State the integer range and acknowledge the verifier’s tolerance of additional signed members.

4. **F4 — MAJOR: the decision-record candidate rules are incompletely restated.**

   **RFC, L162–164:** “A decision record is **cited by the digest of its bytes**: for a whole file, the file; for a `.jsonl` book, each line without its newline, exactly as the verifier enumerates candidates (§4 step 6).”

   **Source:** Step 6 includes **every regular file whole**, including `.jsonl` files, plus additional line candidates for `.jsonl`. It splits on `0x0A`, strips exactly one trailing `0x0D`, skips empty pieces and retains a nonempty unterminated final piece. Step 7 differs: it examines `.jsonl` lines, **not the whole `.jsonl` file**. The byte-rule test explicitly covers these distinctions. [Gateway SPEC §4](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/SPEC.md#L427), [test](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/go/verify_v3_test.go#L267).

   **Smallest edit:** State those rules, including “additionally” for line candidates. Qualify L184’s “each candidate” to preserve step 7’s exclusion of whole `.jsonl` files.

5. **F5 — MAJOR: line digests do not establish an append-only book.**

   **RFC, L240–242:** “Digesting the bytes makes the book append-only in fact: a rewritten line breaks the join, which is the intended consequence.”

   **Source:** Step 6 asks whether **some candidate anywhere in the directory** has the named digest. It binds neither a line’s position nor the book’s sequence. Consequently, reordering individually cited lines preserves their digests; retaining an original candidate elsewhere can preserve its join after another copy is rewritten. These are direct consequences of the specified candidate search. [Gateway SPEC §4 step 6](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/SPEC.md#L427).

   **Smallest edit:** Replace with “Changing a cited candidate’s bytes breaks that digest match if no matching candidate remains in the directory; this does not establish append-only book history.” Correct the repeated claim at L334–335 and qualify L267 accordingly.

6. **F6 — MAJOR: the receipt alone cannot reveal the committed query.**

   **RFC, L139–140:** “The property this buys, and its bound: a reader can answer *which query, which snapshot, through what* off the receipt, offline.”

   **Source:** `acquisition.statement` is a salted commitment or `null`. Revealing its value requires the value and salt; the salt is returned separately and never retained in the store. `snapshot` can also be `null`. [Gateway SPEC §1.2a](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/SPEC.md#L148).

   **Smallest edit:** Say the receipt exposes the reported adapter information and any reported snapshot; a committed statement can be checked offline **when its value and salt are separately disclosed**.

7. **F7 — MAJOR: the adapter’s inability to sign is conditional.**

   **RFC, L140–142:** “The acquisition record is the **adapter's testimony under the gateway's signature** — a compromised adapter can misreport its acquisition as it can misreport its bytes, and is attributable by the source it was configured as; what it cannot do is sign.”

   **Source:** The corresponding gateway paragraph expressly conditions that claim on the adapter being unable to read the seed. An adapter running as the signer’s identity can read what the signer can. [Gateway SPEC §1.2a](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/SPEC.md#L205).

   **Smallest edit:** Append “provided the operator-established isolation prevents it from reading the signing seed.”

8. **F8 — MAJOR: the confidentiality claim covers too much of the receipt.**

   **RFC, L261–263:** “The statement and the request sent to a target are commitments; the salts are returned to the caller and never stored, so a party holding the store learns nothing about them from the receipt.”

   **Source:** The gateway limits this claim to learning nothing **from the commitment**. Other receipt fields—including `endpoint`, `snapshot`, `peerIdentity` and `upstreamToken`—remain cleartext and can disclose information an adapter puts there. [Gateway SPEC §1.2a](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/SPEC.md#L160), [cleartext qualification](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/SPEC.md#L212).

   **Smallest edit:** Replace “from the receipt” with “from their commitments alone”; add that other receipt fields and retained artifacts may disclose those values.

9. **F9 — MAJOR: “every part … merged and released” is false.**

   **RFC, L74:** “Every part named here is merged and released; none of it is a sketch.”

   **Source:** Demo PR #70 is open and unmerged. The RFC itself describes open demo PRs at L108. Locally, gateway tag `v0.2.0` points to `9fdd9a3`, preceding the executor commit; `origin/main` alone establishes merged source, not a release containing it. Runtime `v0.21.0`, conversely, exists at the inspected runtime head. [Demo PR #70](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-demo/pull/70).

   **Smallest edit:** Replace the sentence with “Implementation, release and demonstration status are stated separately for each item below.” Identify release versions only where established.

10. **F10 — MAJOR: the worked-example evidence is stronger and more complete than the available sources establish.**

    **RFC, L104–109:** “**Worked examples** — the demo's Act 7 (a draft pack replayed against a ledger of thirteen past decisions whose rows cite the page receipt they were transcribed under) and Act 8 (a receipted read, a judgment citing it, an action minted under a person's token, the three ledgers verified together, and the judgment rewritten after the fact to show the action receipt outliving it), both in pull requests open in the demo repository at the time of writing.”

    **Source:** PR #70 contains thirteen matrix rows, initially **without citations**. Its optional helper copies the last receipt of the lexicographically last session onto every row; it does not establish that the receipt contains the originating page or those rows. The script is at `projects/enterprise-demo/DEMO.md`, not repository-root `DEMO.md` as L216 names. I found no Act 8 PR in the available public search results, so its asserted standing remains unsubstantiated. [Act 7 script](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-demo/blob/5e2f9a25b417f2489fff0a4ccf404db429c8dcb1/projects/enterprise-demo/DEMO.md), [citation helper](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-demo/blob/5e2f9a25b417f2489fff0a4ccf404db429c8dcb1/scripts/cite-history.py).

    **Smallest edit:** Describe Act 7 as thirteen fixture rows with an optional caller-asserted citation added by the helper; correct the path and link PR #70. Remove Act 8 from completed/open-PR evidence until an identifiable source supports it.

11. **F11 — MINOR: retrospective eligibility under RFC 0000 is assumed rather than established.**

    **RFC, L21–24:** “It is recorded under [RFC 0000's](0000-rfc-process.md) cross-project exploratory provision: a disposition here endorses (or declines) the design record as written and confers no authority over any of those repositories, each of which decides by its own process.”

    **Source:** RFC 0000 expressly describes visibility “before any implementation exists.” RFCs 0010 and 0011 were introduced as prospective records. Their ownership and acceptance discipline supports that part of 0014, but does not expressly resolve eligibility for a newly filed retrospective record.

    **Smallest edit:** Say this record **seeks application** of that provision to retrospective documentation, and list whether the provision covers that case as an unresolved process question. Keep the existing ownership and acceptance limits.

12. **F12 — MINOR: “interprets nothing” omits the deliberate `cites` exception.**

    **RFC, L38–41:** “A verifier that reads the gateway's store and the runtime's decision-record directory side by side then resolves the join in both directions, by digest and by exact name, and interprets nothing it resolves.”

    **Source:** Step 7 reads candidate JSON, identifies top-level `cites`, validates its structure and resolves its entries. The gateway explicitly calls it the one record member the verifier reads. [Gateway SPEC §4 step 7](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/SPEC.md#L440).

    **Smallest edit:** Replace the final clause with “and interprets no decision semantics beyond reading and validating the record’s `cites` member.” Apply the same qualification to the section title and L195.

13. **F13 — MINOR: the identity paragraph excludes valid null callers.**

    **RFC, L200–202:** “`caller` on every receipt and `requester` on an action receipt are the issuer, subject and token digest of a token the gateway verified against a configured issuer, with a local copy of its keys and nothing fetched on the request path.”

    **Source:** Without configured identity, version 3 acquisition receipts carry `caller: null`; actions are refused. Version 2 receipts have no `caller` member. [Gateway identity configuration](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/docs/design/engine-config.md), [SPEC §1.2a](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/SPEC.md#L170).

    **Smallest edit:** Begin “A non-null `caller` on a version 3 receipt, and `requester` on an action receipt…” and state the no-identity null/refusal behavior.

14. **F14 — MINOR: PR #147’s nineteen-findings total is not reconciled with its itemized record.**

    **RFC, L101–103:** “ADR-0033 shipped in runtime 0.20.0 and ADR-0034 is merged for 0.21.0, each with cross-vendor review records on its pull request (145: eight rounds, thirty-one findings; 147: seven rounds, nineteen findings).”

    **Source:** Network access through the GitHub connector succeeded. PR #147’s closing comment reports nineteen, but its disposition tables contain `4 + 2 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 2 = 17` numbered findings; round 7 reports none. The source therefore contains an unresolved discrepancy. [PR #147 discussion](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-runtime/pull/147), [closing summary](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-runtime/pull/147#issuecomment-5659061913).

    **Smallest edit:** Omit #147’s finding total pending reconciliation, or explicitly attribute nineteen to the closing summary and disclose the seventeen itemized findings. Do not silently replace one unsupported total with another interpretation.

All ten required sections are present. The index row correctly matches the title, Draft status and outside-JPS scope. Repository ownership and acceptance-as-endorsement are otherwise consistent with RFCs 0010 and 0011.

The golden-record agreement appears as a design requirement, with no corresponding implementation found in the inspected gateway tree. Gateway PR #114’s **7 rounds/27 findings** and runtime PR #145’s **8 rounds/31 findings** match their itemized discussions.

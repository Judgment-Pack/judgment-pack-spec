### Complete output (verbatim, `.review-scratch/findings.md` as the reviewer saved it)

1. **HIGH — Version-2 same-answer compatibility is broken.** `spec/dialect.md:458`; `rfcs/0018-dialect.md:160`. A correctly signed MCP receipt carrying `pageItems`, with its preparation recomputed under the accepted rules, receives a successful reading from **both origin/main readers**. Both head readers return `acquisition-invalid` for those identical inputs. The ordinary MCP control retains its answer. The RFC explicitly discloses this exception at lines 162–164, but still calls the amendment a “compatible extension”; the requested guarantee that previously valid inputs keep their answers does not hold. **Fix:** version the page restriction if that guarantee is required; otherwise describe and scope this as a breaking version-2 restriction alongside the compatible shape extension, and revise the compatibility claim. **Confirmed by running and reading:** `.review-scratch/probes/page-mcp/`, `probes.log`, `baseline-ts.log`.

2. **MEDIUM — The binding commitment sentence admits an overstated reading.** `spec/dialect.md:399` (especially line 401); mirrored at `rfcs/0018-dialect.md:667`. “A preparation commits it” can mean the binding document, including its `dialect` digest. However, §4.4 removes the unbound metadata and carries neither the binding digest nor the unbound digest into the preparation. Changing only `platform` and updating the binding’s `dialect` produces different unbound and binding documents with **identical bound canonical bytes and digest**. A record binding that preparation cannot distinguish those published dialect identities. **Fix:** explicitly say that the preparation commits the resulting bound document/profile assignments, and does not commit the binding document’s digest, the published dialect’s digest, or the removed catalog metadata. If those identities should be authenticated, carry their digests in the committed format instead. **Confirmed by reading and a hand-built transformation probe:** `.review-scratch/binding/{unbound,binding,other-unbound,other-binding,bound,direct}.json`; `probes.py`.

## Findings (verbatim, unedited)

1. RC-1 — BLOCKER — Scope note; Implementation; Unresolved #5. RFC 0000 permits a cross-project exploratory record only when every proposed part lands in another repository and each destination is named; RFC 0012 instead offers “the runtime, or a dedicated repository,” leaves that choice explicitly undecided, names no witness-side writer or conformance-corpus home, and identifies no concrete first consumer repository. This repeats RFC 0011 round 1’s R-2 placement blocker. Fix: name one external repository for the format, writer, verifier, and vectors, plus each initial consumer repository, leaving later relocation to those repositories’ processes. (`rfcs/0012-witness-contract.md`, scope note, Conformance, Implementation, Unresolved #5; `rfcs/0000-rfc-process.md`, “Cross-project exploratory records”; `rfcs/reviews/0011-round-1.md`, R-2 and disposition.)

2. RC-2 — MAJOR — Summary; Evidence; Security and privacy. The honest evidence ceiling is contradicted elsewhere: “provably cannot,” “measured exactly this,” and the claim that satisfying all six clauses makes equivocation observable promote finite prototype outcomes into proof that the list is exhaustive and sufficient. Study 017’s main stratum was locked replication over behavior observed during development, only its reviewer holdout was prospective, and its non-claims expressly exclude interoperability, transport, discovery, retention policy, incentives, and organizational independence. Fix: disclose the two strata’s evidentiary standing, qualify every result as applying within the registered cells and prototype, and describe the six clauses as non-exhaustive candidates rather than a proven sufficient contract. (`rfcs/0012-witness-contract.md`, Summary, Evidence, Security and privacy; [Study 017 `PREREGISTRATION.md` §§1a, 4c, 9](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/017-witnessed-currency/PREREGISTRATION.md); [Study 017 `ANALYSIS.md`, “Claims and non-claims”](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/017-witnessed-currency/ANALYSIS.md).)

3. RC-3 — MAJOR — Specification §1, Attribution. The “corollary” that a genuine unpinned-witness record is indistinguishable from a corrupted pinned-witness record is a property of Study 017’s schema, not verification generally: its comparator tries records only against configured pinned keys and carries no authenticated candidate signer key or certificate. Another contract could distinguish a valid-but-untrusted signer from an invalid claimed signer without trusting a self-declared routing label. Fix: scope the indistinguishability statement to the prototype and leave authenticated signer material, key-to-witness authority mapping, rotation, and revocation open for the eventual contract. (`rfcs/0012-witness-contract.md`, Specification §1 and Security “The trust root multiplies”; [Study 017 `witness/SPEC.md` §2](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/017-witnessed-currency/witness/SPEC.md); `rfcs/0010-gateway-signing-identity.md`, Specification §3.)

4. RC-4 — MINOR — Specification §1, Attribution. “The label could not cause a false refusal, only a false acceptance” reverses the cited review record. Study 017 R1-4 says the discarded draft argument was that label routing could cause refusal but never acceptance; the reviewer then demonstrated that it could also cause false acceptance. The reproduced construction establishes false acceptance, not impossibility of false refusal. Fix: delete the exclusivity claim and state only that relabelling the honest conflicting record made the draft comparator pass. (`rfcs/0012-witness-contract.md`, Specification §1; [Study 017 `PREREG-REVIEW.md`, Round 1 R1-4](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/017-witnessed-currency/PREREG-REVIEW.md); [Study 017 `harness/MATRIX.json`, `neg-relabel-attack`](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/017-witnessed-currency/harness/MATRIX.json).)

5. RC-5 — MINOR — Specification §2, Delivery. The measured boundary is narrower than “all three yield a pass” and “silence and suppression are the same observation.” Registered endpoint cells executed omission and signature corruption only, under a configuration where another record satisfied the count floor; corruption remained observable as `unattributedSightings: 1`. Fresh-key re-signing appears in registered prose but was not a separate executed endpoint, while a named-witness floor can turn the same absence into refusal. Fix: identify omission and corruption as the measured cells, label re-signing as an inference from this comparator, bind outcomes to their exact enforcement configuration, and limit the indistinguishability claim to the causes of an absent record. (`rfcs/0012-witness-contract.md`, Specification §§2–3; [Study 017 matrix, suppression and required-witness cells](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/017-witnessed-currency/harness/MATRIX.json); [published detection matrix](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/017-witnessed-currency/results/primary-attempt-001/DETECTION-MATRIX.md).)

6. RC-6 — MAJOR — Summary; Implementation; Unresolved #2. RFC 0012 claims to narrow every contract term in RFC 0011 #9 but omits retention: observation coverage and cadence do not specify how long sightings remain durable, retrievable, or auditable. Study 017 deliberately renamed its former “retention horizon” construction to positional prefix coverage after R1-8 and expressly says retention policy was not measured. Unresolved #2 then answers itself by declaring coverage and liveness “contract terms, not mechanism outputs,” although other mechanisms could emit auditable cadence or retention evidence. Fix: add a separate, explicitly unmeasured retention clause and neutrally ask what publication, time, monitoring, and audit mechanisms could evidence retention, coverage, and liveness. (`rfcs/0012-witness-contract.md`, Summary, Implementation, Unresolved #2; `rfcs/0011-judgment-currency-anchor.md`, Unresolved #9; [Study 017 `PREREGISTRATION.md` §4c](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/017-witnessed-currency/PREREGISTRATION.md); [Study 017 `PREREG-REVIEW.md`, R1-8](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/017-witnessed-currency/PREREG-REVIEW.md); `rfcs/reviews/0011-round-5.md`, RB-2/RB-4/RB-5.)

7. RC-7 — MINOR — Specification §4, Coverage. “A record constrains one position” and “one point in a history” understate the authenticated object. A head at position \(n\) commits through predecessor hashes to the entire prefix ending at \(n\); Study 017’s cell established only that a divergence above that horizon remains invisible. This also conflicts with RFC 0011’s reviewed statement that a head binds one prefix unambiguously. Fix: say a sighting constrains the authenticated prefix through one position but says nothing about later positions. (`rfcs/0012-witness-contract.md`, Specification §4; [Study 017 `MATRIX.json`, `wit-prefix-coverage`](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/017-witnessed-currency/harness/MATRIX.json); [Study 016 `registry/SPEC.md` §§1, 3](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/016-policy-currency-anchor/registry/SPEC.md); `rfcs/reviews/0011-round-5.md`, RB-7 and disposition.)

8. RC-8 — MAJOR — Specification §6; Implementation; Unresolved #1. Study 017 supports only that its single-key, isolated-view comparator implements no non-collusion clause; its reviewed wording deliberately says “every implemented clause” and that the cells show why such a clause may be required. RFC 0012 upgrades this to “not a mechanism property at all,” “no mechanism implements,” and “no implementation anywhere,” while answering Unresolved #1 in governance-only terms. Named multi-witness quorums, cross-signing, and collective cosigning can mechanically raise the collusion threshold or expose statements to several witnesses, even though organizational independence remains a governance assumption; CoSi includes a working witness-cosigning prototype. Fix: distinguish mechanized quorum/accountability from the external independence assumption and leave their adequacy open. (`rfcs/0012-witness-contract.md`, Specification §6, Implementation, Unresolved #1; [Study 017 `PREREG-REVIEW.md`, R1-7](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/017-witnessed-currency/PREREG-REVIEW.md); [Study 017 collusion cells and `wit-one-honest`](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/017-witnessed-currency/harness/MATRIX.json); `rfcs/0010-gateway-signing-identity.md`, Alternatives “Threshold signing”; [Syta et al., CoSi](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.08768).)

9. RC-9 — MAJOR — Alternatives, “Per-verifier persisted state only.” RFC 0012 overstates Study 016 as measuring a verifier that persisted or “remembered” state and calls the alternative “strictly cheaper.” The study supplied `minimumHeadPin` as caller-provisioned input; its layer has no storage, returns no state update, and explicitly leaves atomic persistence and update-on-accept as future work. It measured conditional prefix-containment refusal given prior state, and measured no cost comparison. Fix: state that exact result, disclose the unimplemented persistence lifecycle, and remove or qualify the cost ordering. (`rfcs/0012-witness-contract.md`, Alternatives; [Study 016 `registry/SPEC.md` §§2–3](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/016-policy-currency-anchor/registry/SPEC.md); [Study 016 `ANALYSIS.md`, “Detection ownership”](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/016-policy-currency-anchor/ANALYSIS.md).)

10. RC-10 — MAJOR — Alternatives, “A full transparency log.” “Strictly stronger and strictly heavier” imports RFC 0010’s ordering outside its actual comparator: RFC 0010 compares an external log with gateway checkpoints and its self-contained localhost deployment, not with every possible witness architecture. “A log has witnesses” is also false categorically; a bare transparency log has an operator and clients/monitors, and inclusion or consistency proofs do not by themselves expose split views to an isolated client. Fix: describe the stronger within-view inclusion/prefix machinery, make cost deployment-dependent, and separately require state, gossip, monitoring, or optional witness cosigning for cross-view observability. (`rfcs/0012-witness-contract.md`, Alternatives; `rfcs/0010-gateway-signing-identity.md`, Specification §4 and Alternatives; `rfcs/0011-judgment-currency-anchor.md`, Unresolved #9; `rfcs/reviews/0011-round-3.md`, RA-2/RA-3; [RFC 9162 §§1, 8.3, 11.3](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9162.html).)

11. RC-11 — MAJOR — Compatibility. RFC 0012 misstates RFC 0011’s conclusion as requiring every adopting verifier to receive a versioned protocol envelope. RFC 0011 says an envelope is necessary to make a witness- or currency-unaware verifier reject instead of silently skipping the new step; an adopting verifier can instead be configured out of band to require witness input, exactly as Study 017’s `minimumSightings` and `requiredWitnesses` do. Fix: restore the unaware-reader qualifier and leave protocol-envelope signaling versus verifier-local required-input configuration open per consumer. (`rfcs/0012-witness-contract.md`, Compatibility; `rfcs/0011-judgment-currency-anchor.md`, Compatibility; [Study 017 `witness/SPEC.md` §§1–2](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/017-witnessed-currency/witness/SPEC.md).)

12. RC-12 — MAJOR — Implementation. The heading does not satisfy RFC 0000’s adapted implementation requirement: it names one same-project study harness and one prospective complementary consumer step, but not two plausible independent implementations of the witness contract. Study 017 itself disclaims interoperability and format-proposal status, while the prospective consumer is neither named nor an independent writer/verifier implementation. Fix: enumerate two concrete independently implementable producer/verifier paths, name their repositories, state which clauses each exercises, and retain the caveat that two implementations from this project establish precision rather than interoperability. (`rfcs/0012-witness-contract.md`, Implementation; `rfcs/0000-rfc-process.md`, “Required sections,” item 9; `rfcs/0010-gateway-signing-identity.md`, Implementation; `rfcs/0011-judgment-currency-anchor.md`, Implementation; [Study 017 `PREREGISTRATION.md` §9](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/017-witnessed-currency/PREREGISTRATION.md).)

13. RC-13 — MINOR — Conformance; Security “Resource risk.” The proposed cases erase configurations that determine their outcomes: an omitted record passes only when surviving evidence satisfies the count floor, and an unattributable record can still lead to refusal when a count or named-witness floor is unmet. The list also omits Study 017’s resource-limit control and supplies no at-limit/one-past-limit vectors despite Security requiring fail-closed limits. Fix: restate the suppression, required-witness, and zero-sighting cases as exact paired configurations with structured evidence, and add record-size, set-size, and verification-work cases at and beyond each limit. (`rfcs/0012-witness-contract.md`, Specification §§2–3, Conformance, Security; [Study 017 registered matrix and `neg-limits`](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/017-witnessed-currency/harness/MATRIX.json); `rfcs/0000-rfc-process.md`, required sections 7–8; `rfcs/reviews/0011-round-1.md`, R-13 and disposition.)

14. RC-14 — MINOR — Security and privacy, “Disclosure.” Saying a record reveals which view an actor “observed” promotes a signed assertion into evidence that the observation occurred, contrary to the record’s binding-not-truth ceiling. Study 017’s bounded result is which histories pinned witness keys attested or recorded having seen; it establishes neither real-world observation nor actor identity. Fix: say the record reveals the head a witness key attested to, then retain the existing audience, persistence, and correlation analysis. (`rfcs/0012-witness-contract.md`, scope note and Security “Disclosure”; [Study 017 `ANALYSIS.md`, “Claims and non-claims”](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/017-witnessed-currency/ANALYSIS.md); [Study 017 `witness/SPEC.md` §4](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/017-witnessed-currency/witness/SPEC.md); `rfcs/reviews/0011-round-5.md`, RB-6.)

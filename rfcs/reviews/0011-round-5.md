## Findings (verbatim, unedited)

1. RB-1 — MAJOR — Section: Summary, Specification §2a, Security “freshness floor.” Section 2a makes snapshot membership only an input to continued reliance, yet the Summary still calls a reused decision under a retired pack “simply out of date,” and Security says a pre-snapshot retirement is “caught.” Under §2a’s own grace and grandfathering examples, only the tuple’s nonmembership is universal; usability is unresolved. Fix: describe the tuple solely as “not current at the observed snapshot” and defer continued reliance explicitly to an external transition rule. (`rfcs/0011-judgment-currency-anchor.md`, Summary, Specification §§2–2a, Security and privacy; `rfcs/reviews/0011-round-1.md`, R-7 disposition.)

2. RB-2 — MAJOR — Section: Specification §2a. “Belongs to the relying organization,” “not to the consumer step,” “none … is more correct,” and “whoever bears the risk” go beyond scope narrowing: they categorically assign governance and implementation ownership. A rule may be constrained by an external legal, contractual, or issuing authority, and consumer software may apply a configured rule while preserving the raw membership result. Core assigns no such transition authority. Fix: state only that this registry and §2’s membership primitive do not determine continued reliance; leave the rule’s source, binding force, placement, and evaluation open. (`rfcs/0011-judgment-currency-anchor.md`, Specification §2a and Unresolved #10; `spec/judgment-pack-core.md`, §§5, 10, 11, 13; `rfcs/reviews/0011-round-3.md`, RA-1–RA-3 dispositions.)

3. RB-3 — MINOR — Section: Specification §§1 and 2a. “The format … carries no expiry … semantics” is facially broader than intended because §1’s `retire` event makes a version leave the supported set at a defined position. Fix: say the format carries no “decision-level expiry, grace, or grandfathering semantics” or no semantics for continued reliance on an existing decision. (`rfcs/0011-judgment-currency-anchor.md`, Specification §§1 and 2a.)

4. RB-4 — MAJOR — Section: Specification §2a and Unresolved #10. The “non-binding hint” option is not genuinely open under §2a: a recommended grace or transition alongside retirement necessarily carries transition semantics, which §2a categorically excludes. Calling hardening “the failure mode this separation exists to prevent” argues against the option inside the question, while “an authority” assumes the currency signer may recommend transition policy despite Unresolved #1 distinguishing publishers from neutral logs that attest only inclusion. Fix: remove the registry-hint branch, or limit §2a’s prohibition to authoritative/enforced transition semantics and neutrally ask who issues, signs, labels, and owns optional advice. (`rfcs/0011-judgment-currency-anchor.md`, Specification §2a, Security and privacy, Unresolved #1/#10; `rfcs/reviews/0011-round-1.md`, R-2/R-9 dispositions; `rfcs/0000-rfc-process.md`, “Cross-project exploratory records.”)

5. RB-5 — MAJOR — Section: Unresolved #10. The closing “asymmetry” is a categorical answer and is unsound at its stated breadth. Section 2 already calls membership a “verdict”; membership at one snapshot cannot by itself serve grace or grandfathering rules needing the trusted ordering that §2 and #3 say is absent, while a parameterized verifier can expose raw membership and calculate more than one separately labeled derived result. Fix: delete the sentence or say conditionally that exposing raw membership avoids hard-coding one rule, while asking what additional evidence and rule configuration derived evaluation requires. (`rfcs/0011-judgment-currency-anchor.md`, Specification §2, Unresolved #3/#10/#11; `rfcs/reviews/0011-round-3.md`, RA-1–RA-3 dispositions.)

6. RB-6 — MAJOR — Section: Unresolved #11. The claimed two-sided “fact” that an artifact was “created against” a head contradicts the later admission that the artifact proves only what its author claims to have relied on. Calling the citation “precisely” what a transition rule needs and saying it gives fail-fast also overclaims: immediate-retirement rules need no historical citation, grace/grandfathering rules need ordering the citation lacks, and mint-time refusal requires a separately chosen transition and accepted-head/freshness policy. Study 014 was reachable and supports only the narrower conclusion that an attacker-controlled internal field cannot establish currency. Fix: say the signed artifact cites a head where `v1` was current, call that one possible input, and make rejection conditional on separately specified policy. (`rfcs/0011-judgment-currency-anchor.md`, Specification §§2–2a, Unresolved #3/#10/#11; [Study 014 `PREREGISTRATION.md` §4c](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/014-openworkproof-binding/PREREGISTRATION.md#4c-analytic-limitations-not-empirical-rows); `rfcs/reviews/0011-round-1.md`, R-7 disposition.)

7. RB-7 — MINOR — Section: Unresolved #11, split-view limit. A registry head binds one canonical prefix, so split views do not make that cited head ambiguous; they prevent an isolated verifier from establishing that the named view is globally unique or canonical, or from discovering sibling views. Fix: use #9’s exact bound instead of “only as unambiguous as the history it names.” (`rfcs/0011-judgment-currency-anchor.md`, Specification §1 and Unresolved #9/#11; `rfcs/reviews/0011-round-3.md`, RA-3 disposition.)

8. RB-8 — MINOR — Section: Problem and Unresolved #11, public-thread attribution. Problem still calls the remark “independent” and “not a citable public artifact,” while #11 calls it public and claims its citation establishes “by whom.” The supplied link is the thread root rather than an exact comment and the RFC supplies no commenter handle, date, or observable wording; the DEV page could not be verified through the available web access, so the attribution cannot be confirmed. Fix: reconcile Problem, remove the independence claim, and cite the exact dated comment permalink with only the verified public handle and a narrow paraphrase—or remove the attribution. (`rfcs/0011-judgment-currency-anchor.md`, Problem and Unresolved #11; `rfcs/reviews/0011-round-1.md`, R-11 disposition; [cited DEV thread](https://dev.to/kikashy/the-receipt-was-valid-the-policy-was-retired-164a).)

9. RB-9 — MINOR — Section: Security and privacy / Unresolved #11. Existing disclosure analysis covers lifecycle history visible to snapshot readers, but #11 would place a stable head identifier in every signed decision and execution artifact, potentially exposing an actor’s observed view to a wider audience, correlating artifacts that share a head, and retaining that linkage beyond registry access controls. Fix: add the citation’s audience, retention, correlation, and head-resolution risks to Security or #11. (`rfcs/0011-judgment-currency-anchor.md`, Security “Metadata disclosure” and Unresolved #11; `rfcs/0000-rfc-process.md`, Required sections item 7.)

mergeable after listed fixes

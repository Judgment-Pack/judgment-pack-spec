The full reread produced these findings:

1. **R3-F1 — MAJOR: the Summary promotes attestation into proof of source origin.**

   **Exact sentence, RFC 0014 L37–39:**

   > The gateway's receipts prove which bytes a named source returned; the runtime's decision record says what was judged and decided; a write to a target system was the application's own log.

   **Source:** Gateway SPEC §5 expressly limits the signature to byte-lineage under an operator-configured authority label. It does not establish that the genuinely named source returned those bytes. SECURITY.md makes the same distinction and explicitly permits fabricated source responses to produce valid receipts. The adapter-testimony qualification later in RFC 0014 does not make this opening proof claim true. [SPEC §5](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/SPEC.md#L518), [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/SECURITY.md#L67).

   **Smallest edit:** Replace the first clause with:

   > The gateway's receipts bind retained bytes to its attestation under operator-configured source and authority labels;

2. **R3-F2 — MINOR: Evidence still excludes the runtime’s document-size condition.**

   **Exact sentence, RFC 0014 L107–110:**

   > **The record's citation** — runtime ADR-0033: `cites` on every record a run leaves, held to the gateway's structural grammar and to nothing else, recorded as given, omitted when none was supplied; a rehearsal accepts a citations document of the grammar and within the limit and writes no record, citations included.

   **Source:** `ParseCites` rejects a document exceeding `1 << 20` bytes before parsing. Its boundary test demonstrates that valid JSON whitespace alone can take an otherwise valid document over that limit. The corrected rehearsal clause does not qualify the preceding general claim. [Parser](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-runtime/blob/0c8a4b6/internal/audit/audit.go#L180), [boundary test](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-runtime/blob/0c8a4b6/internal/audit/audit_test.go#L587).

   **Smallest edit:** Replace “held to the gateway's structural grammar and to nothing else” with:

   > held to the gateway's structural grammar and the runtime's one-MiB document limit, without resolving or verifying citations

3. **R3-F3 — MINOR: offline commitment checking still incorrectly requires disclosure by the caller.**

   **Exact sentence, RFC 0014 L160–163:**

   > The property this buys, and its bound: a reader can take *through what* — the adapter by name and digest, the shape, the endpoint — and *which snapshot*, when the source reported one, off the receipt offline; *which query* is a commitment, checkable offline only when the caller discloses the statement and its salt, and `null` when the adapter reported none.

   **Source:** Checking the commitment requires the statement and salt; it does not require that the caller supplied them. The gateway explicitly acknowledges that another party may obtain the salt by reading the plaintext exchange. Someone who obtains both values can check the commitment without any caller disclosure. This is the residual counterpart of R2-F1. [Commitment construction](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/SPEC.md#L148), [custody qualification](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/03582d9/SECURITY.md#L86).

   **Smallest edit:** Replace “only when the caller discloses the statement and its salt” with:

   > when the statement and its salt are available to the reader

All ten required sections are present. Subject to those corrections, their treatment is consistent with the exploratory scope: repository ownership is explicit, acceptance endorses only the record, independent implementation evidence is expressly unmet, and retrospective eligibility remains an unresolved process question. The index matches the RFC. RFC 0003’s implementation bar is correctly described: two adapter shapes do not demonstrate two runtimes resolving the same pack references.

All twenty store vectors exist, and the named statuses, citation bounds, candidate-byte rules, identity behavior and version claims are supported. The golden-record test appears as a design requirement; I found no implementation in the inspected gateway tree.

Network access succeeded, so the PR counts were independently checked: [gateway #114](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/pull/114) has **7 rounds/27 findings**; [runtime #145](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-runtime/pull/145) has **8/31**; [runtime #147](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-runtime/pull/147#issuecomment-5659061913) has **7 rounds/17 itemized findings**, with **19** asserted in its closing summary. The RFC reports that discrepancy accurately.

**Final verdict: `mergeable after fixes` — narrow the source-origin claim, remove the remaining grammar-only claim, and remove the caller-only disclosure condition.**

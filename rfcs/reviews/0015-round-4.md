**None.** I found no additional BLOCKER, MAJOR or MINOR requiring an edit. The clean pass rests on these checks:

The runtime’s caller-assertion boundary and exact three-member input grammar are accurately described. [Runtime parser](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-runtime/blob/0c8a4b6/internal/audit/audit.go#L185)

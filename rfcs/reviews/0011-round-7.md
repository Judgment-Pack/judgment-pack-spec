## Round 7 — findings (verbatim) and dispositions

### R7-1 — MINOR

> Offending diff text:
>
> "it evidences that §1's sketch can be implemented and exercised offline"
>
> Contradicting merged text, `rfcs/0011-judgment-currency-anchor.md:136-142`:
>
> "What is unproven is every part the currency problem adds: content-bound history, a
> lifecycle state machine, and an authority for the assertion."
>
> "What is unproven is the signed, third-party pinnable, versioned-with-history
> generalization — which is most of the design."
>
> The feasibility claim is supported at study-internal strength, but it leaves the RFC's
> Evidence section internally stale. Proposed fix: Update Evidence to record that one
> study-internal schema was exercised while the final portable format, authority governance,
> independent implementation, and interoperability remain unproven.

**Disposition: accepted.** A fourth Evidence bullet is added — "The study line exercised one
registered schema, at study-internal strength" — recording the exercised schema and restating
the unproven remainder (the portable format §0 waits on, Unresolved #1's authority governance,
independent implementation, interoperability), which the studies' own registrations disclaim.

### R7-2 — MAJOR

> Offending diff text:
>
> "a duration-measured window is unavailable offline, exactly #3's gap"
>
> Contradicting source, `studies/018-transition-rules/PREREGISTRATION.md:142-149`:
>
> "Ordering is out of reach by construction, so no cell measures elapsed time and the window
> rule is positional by necessity."
>
> The apparatus-specific reason is stated in `studies/018-transition-rules/ANALYSIS.md:46-50`:
>
> "The only ordering available offline is positional; `effectiveFrom` is carried and never
> compared in the pinned upstream, and nothing here holds a clock."
>
> The categorical wording also narrows `rfcs/0011-judgment-currency-anchor.md:374-378`:
>
> "Whether real-time staleness is irreducibly online — and therefore outside the line's
> offline-first posture — is open"
>
> Proposed fix: Say that this no-clock apparatus returned `transition-unavailable` for its
> duration-window cell while leaving open whether another offline design could verify retained
> trusted ordering.

**Disposition: accepted.** #10's parenthetical now reads: the duration-window cell "returned
`transition-unavailable` in that no-clock apparatus — exhibiting #3's gap there, without
deciding whether another offline design could verify a retained trusted ordering." The
categorical "unavailable offline" is withdrawn; Unresolved #3 stays exactly as open as the
merged text left it.

### R7-3 — MAJOR

> Offending diff text:
>
> "The same study measured, within its registered cells, what a citation buys: … signing the
> citation changes nothing"
>
> Contradicting source, `studies/018-transition-rules/rule/SPEC.md:26-34`:
>
> "It is **unsigned here on purpose**"
>
> The executed comparison in
> `studies/018-transition-rules/results/primary-attempt-001/DETECTION-MATRIX.md:54-57`
> establishes only:
>
> "**div-grandfather-on-cited-support, bnd-backdated-citation** — byte-identical cells: same
> observed outcomes"
>
> Thus the signing conclusion is a registered analytic inference from the full-keys model, not
> an executed signed-versus-unsigned result. Proposed fix: State that the unsigned cells were
> byte-identical and separately label author-signing as an unexecuted inference that would not
> close the backdating gap.

**Disposition: accepted.** #11's parenthetical now separates the two: the byte-identity of the
honest and backdated cells is the executed result; the citation "is unsigned in that apparatus
on purpose"; and the conclusion that author-signing would not close the gap — because the
signing party chooses what to cite — is labeled "the study's registered inference from its
full-keys attacker model, not an executed comparison."

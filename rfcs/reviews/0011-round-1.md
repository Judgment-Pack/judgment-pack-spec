## Findings (verbatim, unedited)

1. R-1 — BLOCKER — Section: RFC 0011 “Problem,” “Evidence,” Specification §§1–2, and “Conformance.” Study 014’s `e22` is not an older Judgment Pack: it is a descriptive, no-credit remint under a different OpenWorkProof WorkOrder with the same judgment commitment, so `(packId, packVersion, packDigest)` is unchanged and this registry would accept it. The RFC therefore mischaracterizes the experiment and falsely claims its mechanism catches `e22` ([Study 014 `PREREGISTRATION.md` §4c](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/014-openworkproof-binding/PREREGISTRATION.md#4c-analytic-limitations-not-empirical-rows), [`MATRIX.json` entry `e22`](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/014-openworkproof-binding/harness/MATRIX.json), [`adapter/SPEC.md` §1](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/014-openworkproof-binding/adapter/SPEC.md), [primary detection matrix](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/014-openworkproof-binding/results/primary-attempt-001/DETECTION-MATRIX.md)). Fix: remove `e22` as evidence and as the claimed conformance vector, then either add and run a genuine retired-pack remint case or explicitly broaden the proposal to a separately authorized WorkOrder-currency anchor.

2. R-2 — BLOCKER — Section: RFC 0011 scope note, Specification §3, and Unresolved #4/#6. RFC 0000 permits a cross-project exploratory record only when every proposed part lands in another repository and requires each destination to be named; RFC 0011 instead offers its format as a facet of RFC 0005—a standards-track candidate profile in this repository—or an unnamed sibling, mentions an undefined “JPS-side reference verifier,” and admits the eventual owner is undecided. RFC 0005 also defines only a catalog index, with signing/provenance unresolved; it has no authoritative completeness, currency, retirement, or append-only semantics, so it is not “one field and one signature away” (`rfcs/0000-rfc-process.md`, “Cross-project exploratory records”; `rfcs/0005-pack-discovery.md`, header, “Specification,” and “Unresolved questions”; contrast `rfcs/0010-gateway-signing-identity.md`, scope note). Fix: either reclassify this as standards-track/profile work with exact RFC 0001/0005 amendments, or name a concrete external repository for the format and verifier and remove JPS/profile destinations.

3. R-3 — MAJOR — Section: RFC 0011 “Problem” and “Evidence.” `e18` was not registered or executed in the frozen primary attempt: the governing preregistration says it was removed because it was baseline bytes plus an unobservable scenario, and the 39-row primary matrix contains no `e18`. The post-run `ANALYSIS.md` sentence claiming two registered cells conflicts with the preregistration and pinned artifacts that its own opening says govern ([Study 014 `PREREGISTRATION.md` §4c](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/014-openworkproof-binding/PREREGISTRATION.md#4c-analytic-limitations-not-empirical-rows), [`PREREG-REVIEW.md` R1-10](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/014-openworkproof-binding/PREREG-REVIEW.md), [primary detection matrix](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/014-openworkproof-binding/results/primary-attempt-001/DETECTION-MATRIX.md)). Fix: describe `e18` as an analytic limitation, not a preregistered measured result, and disclose rather than propagate the upstream analysis inconsistency.

4. R-4 — MAJOR — Section: RFC 0011 “Problem,” “Evidence,” and Specification §3. The gateway scope quotation is accurate, but the prior-art claim is overstated. Its seal binds only `(sessionId, finalCount, sealedAt, keyId)` and has no store identity, registry head, prefix hash, or authenticated global position; its “whole-session replay” test merely inserts a never-sealed session and observes `unregistered-session`. A genuinely sealed session accompanied by its valid seal is not distinguishable from the same pair copied elsewhere, and refusing re-seal is writer behavior rather than third-party proof of append-only history ([gateway `SPEC.md` §§2–5](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/main/SPEC.md), [`go/service_test.go`](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/main/go/service_test.go), `CONTRIBUTING.md` “Scope”). Fix: say the gateway demonstrates offline comparison against a separately trusted snapshot and count mismatches relative to it—not authenticated monotonic history or general replay detection—and identify currency log heads, prefix consistency, lifecycle state, and authority as new machinery.

5. R-5 — MAJOR — Section: RFC 0011 “Problem,” “Evidence,” Specification §3, and “Implementation.” The runtime lock is correctly described as local and unsigned, but “the right shape at the wrong scope” and “only the scope change is unproven” are false. `jpack.lock.json` is a replaceable snapshot keyed by project-local entries containing paths and exact-byte digests for configuration, packs, and graphs; it has no Core series identifier, pack version, supported-set membership, retirement, transition history, or append-only order, and its ADR explicitly says it reviews/approves nothing ([runtime ADR-0019, “Decision outcome” and “Consequences”](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-runtime/blob/main/docs/adr/0019-reviewed-set-lock.md), [README “The reviewed set”](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-runtime/blob/main/README.md#the-reviewed-set)). Fix: cite it only as precedent for deterministic local digest inventories and drift refusal; state that the currency writer’s identity, lifecycle, history, signature, and trust model are all new.

6. R-6 — MAJOR — Section: RFC 0011 “Problem,” Specification preface/§1, §3, and “Compatibility.” `contentHash` is not an existing portable primitive. RFC 0001 is Draft and explicitly leaves both digest algorithm and canonicalization unresolved, while Core §13 likewise leaves content identity, canonicalization, and signatures open; Study 014’s `packDigest` is SHA-256 over exact retained bytes, not an agreed canonical-pack hash (`rfcs/0001-pack-manifest.md`, “Specification” and “Unresolved questions”; `spec/judgment-pack-core.md` §13; [Study 014 `adapter/SPEC.md` §1](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/blob/main/studies/014-openworkproof-binding/adapter/SPEC.md)). Letting the registry owner choose canonicalization cannot ensure that chains, manifests, and registries compare the same digest. Fix: make settlement of RFC 0001’s digest scheme a hard dependency or define an algorithm-qualified exact-byte/canonical representation shared by every producer and consumer; qualify “already expressible” and “no format changes.”

7. R-7 — MAJOR — Section: RFC 0011 Specification §2, “Security and privacy” freshness floor, and Unresolved #3. The procedure establishes only membership at one snapshot, not that stale reuse or rollback occurred. A judgment legitimately made and used while `v1` was current but audited after retirement is rejected identically to one reused after retirement; conversely, a use after an old snapshot but before an unseen later retirement passes. A valid signature/date authenticates the authority’s assertion, not that the snapshot is latest, and step 3 contains no persisted minimum position/head to reject replay of an older signed snapshot. Moreover, publication of a newer version is not itself staleness because §1 allows old and new versions to remain concurrently current. Fix: report only “not current at snapshot”; distinguish present-time gating from retrospective audit; define trusted ordering between action and registry state, snapshot high-water/rollback rules, and any clock, expiration, or update-SLA assumptions; define staleness by retirement/non-membership, not publication.

8. R-8 — MAJOR — Section: RFC 0011 Specification §1, “Conformance,” and Unresolved #2. The monotonicity rule conflates append-only history with monotonic version values. Core supplies a three-component revision string and only SHOULD-level immutability; it defines no SemVer precedence, supersession, retirement, branch lineage, or resurrection (`spec/judgment-pack-core.md` §§4, 5, and 11; `rfcs/0001-pack-manifest.md`, optional `supersedes`). A legitimate late `1.4.2` backport after `2.0.0`, or emergency reinstatement of a retired safe release, has the same shape as the proposed “rolled-back entry”: forbidding it breaks real lifecycles, while allowing it means the stated adversarial case is not mechanically distinguishable. Fix: define an explicit add/retire/reinstate event state machine, immutable `(version,digest)` bindings, epochs or branch lineage, and authenticated sequence/prefix rules; make monotonicity a property of history rather than numeric versions.

9. R-9 — MAJOR — Section: RFC 0011 Specification §§1–2, “Security and privacy” signer discussion, and Unresolved #1. “Who signs” is nominally unresolved, but the verifier quietly assumes that pinning a key authorizes it to speak for the referenced `seriesId`; Core’s absolute URI supplies syntax, not authenticated ownership, and Core §10 forbids treating a URL or publisher label as authenticity (`spec/judgment-pack-core.md` §§5 and 10). The two proposed signer models also require different artifacts: publisher signing needs series-to-key delegation, rotation, and revocation, while a neutral log must preserve publisher assertions and can attest inclusion, not currency authority; a log signature alone does not detect publisher equivocation for an isolated offline verifier. Fix: specify the series/namespace-to-authority trust mapping and distinct publisher/log signature layers, including split-view assumptions, or narrow the sketch to one trust model.

10. R-10 — MAJOR — Section: RFC 0011 Specification §2, “Compatibility,” and “Conformance.” The consumer step is neither protocol-agnostic nor purely additive as claimed. Core’s portable disposition contains exactly `kind`, `outcomeId`, `reasons`, and `handoff`, JPS defines no receipt chain, and a protocol may bind opaque judgment bytes without exposing structured `(seriesId, version, contentHash)` (`spec/judgment-pack-core.md` §8.3; `docs/architecture/vision.md`, “Whether other systems respect a judgment…”). OWP carrying the tuple proves only OWP’s suitability. Separately, unchanged artifacts cannot make a currency-unaware verifier fail closed: the proposed “explicit envelope” must itself be a versioned consumer-protocol change, contradicting the claims that artifacts are unchanged and no existing result changes. Fix: scope the sketch to OWP, or admit and specify per-protocol tuple/envelope changes and old-reader behavior.

11. R-11 — MINOR — Section: RFC 0011 “Problem” and “Evidence,” “Independent convergence.” The claimed OpenWorkProof-author comment is not traceable: all named repositories were reachable, but the RFC provides no URL, no Study 014 file preserves the comment, and the reachable [Study 014 PR #49](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-evaluator-experiments/pull/49) timeline contains only maintainer and bot comments. Even a located comment could establish only what the author wrote, not that they lacked access to the study’s reasoning or that two systems independently converged. Fix: cite the exact dated public thread and narrow the claim to its observable wording; otherwise remove it from the evidence section.

12. R-12 — MINOR — Section: RFC 0011 “Summary.” “A judgment authorizes an action” contradicts Core’s explicit rule that an outcome is a declared result, not authorization, and that external execution is outside Core (`spec/judgment-pack-core.md` §§3.5 and 6.4; `docs/architecture/vision.md`, “Whether other systems respect a judgment…”). Later caveats do not cure the load-bearing opening sentence. Fix: say an action is taken in reliance on a judgment under a downstream authorization mapping.

13. R-13 — MINOR — Section: RFC 0011 “Security and privacy” and “Conformance.” Although all ten RFC 0000 headings are present, the security section omits the required resource-risk treatment for an unbounded append-only registry, and the conformance sketch has no snapshot-size, entry-count, current-set-size, or processing-work boundary cases (`rfcs/0000-rfc-process.md`, “Required sections,” item 7). Fix: define implementation limits and fail-closed behavior for oversized snapshots, series, sets, and histories, with boundary and adversarial vectors.

not mergeable as written

## Maintainer dispositions

All thirteen findings **accepted**. The review found two genuine conceptual holes
(R-1, R-2) and eight material overstatements; the revision reframes the record from
"a direction that closes e18/e22" to "an open problem — stale pack-version detection —
and what closing it would actually require," which is the honest character and the one
RFC 0010 already models.

### New finding

#### R6-1 — MINOR: “every vector” overstates what runs through the process contract

**Exact sentence — Implementation, lines 490–493:**

> A second verifier of version 3 now exists: the gateway's `verify-ts`, written in another language from the gateway's `SPEC.md` and frozen corpus, answers to every vector through the corpus's process contract ([gateway #129](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/pull/129)).

**Source/problem:** The process runner exercises `canon.json` and the version 2 and version 3 store vectors. It does not exercise `ed25519-vectors.json`. Those signature vectors are exercised separately by `verify-ts`’s unit test, which calls Node’s cryptographic API directly. Thus the agreement claim is supported, but its stated execution route is too broad. [Process runner](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/9d1625c/go/conform.go#L212), [signature-vector test](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/9d1625c/verify-ts/test/corpus.test.ts#L36).

**Smallest edit:** Replace:

> answers to every vector through the corpus's process contract

with:

> answers to every canonicalization and store vector through the corpus's process contract

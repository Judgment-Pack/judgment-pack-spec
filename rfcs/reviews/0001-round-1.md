## Round 1 — findings (verbatim)

R1-1 — BLOCKER

**Offending diff text:**

> “The expedient is now the rule.”

> “That agreement now exists … the rule is settled … portability here rests on an agreed rule, not on observed agreement.”

**Contradicting sources:**

> “Status: Draft” and “This is an open proposal, not part of the specification.”

— `rfcs/0001-pack-manifest.md:3`, `line 7`

> “Acceptance means the design is approved…”

> Draft RFCs 0001–0007 make questions visible “rather than settled.”

— `rfcs/0000-rfc-process.md:41`, `line 80`

Selecting a concrete candidate and removing its unresolved bullet is compatible with Draft status; claiming an existing agreement is not. RFC 0011 also depends on external producers and consumers that RFC acceptance cannot compel.

**Proposed fix:** Say RFC 0001 “now specifies a concrete candidate rule” and that the algorithm/canonicalization ambiguity is resolved within the proposal, while acceptance and external adoption remain pending. Alternatively, disposition RFC 0001 and establish adoption by the relevant owners before asserting agreement.

R1-2 — MAJOR

**Offending diff text:**

> “the published interoperability studies (014, 016–018) registered SHA-256 over exact retained bytes as an expedient pending this rule”

> “matching … the expedient the studies registered”

**Contradicting sources:**

Study 017 expressly used no pack artifact:

> “No evaluator binary, no external clone, no chains … with synthetic commitment tuples”

— `studies/017-witnessed-currency/PREREGISTRATION.md:107`

Its pack-shaped digest hashes a label:

> `DIGEST_A = "sha256:" + hashlib.sha256(b"study-017/pack-1.0.0").hexdigest()`

— `studies/017-witnessed-currency/harness/build_fixtures.py:38`

Study 018 likewise registers only a commitment tuple, snapshot, trust configuration, citation, and rule configuration, and computes:

> `DIGEST_A = "sha256:" + hashlib.sha256(b"018/pack-1.0.0").hexdigest()`

— `studies/018-transition-rules/PREREGISTRATION.md:60`, `harness/build_fixtures.py:41`

The interoperability label is also expressly disclaimed:

> “This is **not an interoperability study**”

— `studies/017-witnessed-currency/PREREGISTRATION.md:63`

> “No interoperability claim of any kind”

— `studies/018-transition-rules/PREREGISTRATION.md:164`

Only Study 016 explicitly supplies the claimed precedent:

> “`packDigest` (`sha256:` over the exact pack file bytes … an expedient while RFC 0001's digest scheme is unsettled…)”

— `studies/016-policy-currency-anchor/registry/SPEC.md:43`

**Proposed fix:** Narrow both RFCs to Studies 014 and 016: Study 014 registered prefixed SHA-256 over retained pack bytes, and Study 016 carried it into the registry and called it an expedient. Call them “published studies,” not “interoperability studies.” Studies 017–018 reused synthetic digest-shaped tuples and add no retained-pack evidence. Their bare `MANIFEST.sha256` entries are fixture-integrity hashes, not candidate `contentHash` representations.

R1-3 — MAJOR

**Offending diff text:**

> “semantic identity is not a manifest concept, and Core §8.3 already takes this position by making the disposition byte-portable while leaving the pack's representation alone.”

**Contradicting sources:**

Core §8.3 canonicalizes only the disposition:

> “where a byte comparison is required, each disposition is first canonicalized…”

> “Two conforming implementations given the same pack … MUST produce byte-identical canonicalized dispositions. That is the whole of the portability claim.”

— `spec/judgment-pack-core.md:660`, `line 670`

Core leaves pack identity unresolved:

> “content identity, canonicalization, and signatures”

— `spec/judgment-pack-core.md:851`

RFC 0003 confirms:

> “§13 selects no scheme”

— `rfcs/0003-evidence-reference.md:86`

**Proposed fix:** Own exact-byte identity as RFC 0001’s manifest-layer choice. State that Core §8.3 separately canonicalizes dispositions and makes no pack-content-identity claim.

R1-4 — MINOR

**Offending diff text:**

> “Two byte-different packs carry different digests”

**Contradicting definitions:**

> `contentHash` has “64 lowercase hex characters”

> “Digest comparison is exact string equality.”

— `rfcs/0001-pack-manifest.md:44`, `line 53`

Core admits JSON texts subject only to implementation-documented resource limits.

— `spec/judgment-pack-core.md:65`

A fixed 256-bit output cannot be injective over all possible pack byte strings.

**Proposed fix:** Say byte-different packs are hashed as distinct inputs and normally have different digests, or qualify the statement with “absent a SHA-256 collision.”

R1-5 — MINOR

**Offending diff text:**

> “That agreement now exists … the rule is settled”

**Contradicting remaining RFC 0011 text:**

> “What is unproven is unchanged in kind: the portable format §0 waits on…”

— `rfcs/0011-judgment-currency-anchor.md:144`

This is the one remaining passage in the old prerequisite tense.

**Proposed fix:** Rewrite it to identify the actual remaining gap after R1-1—acceptance/adoption of the candidate and independent interoperability evidence—or remove the stale §0 reference.

R1-6 — MINOR

**Offending diff text:**

> “the reference runtime's reviewed-set lock records `sha256:<hex>` over the exact bytes it decoded”

**Contradicting source:**

> “Generate builds the lock … by reading every declared document”

— `internal/lock/lock.go:115`

Generation calls `ReadPack` and digests the returned raw `data`; `ReadPack` itself is only:

> `return p.root.Read(entry.Path, carrier.HardMaxBytes)`

— `internal/lock/lock.go:137`, `internal/project/project.go:543`

**Proposed fix:** Change “the exact bytes it decoded” to “the exact pack bytes it reads and pins.” The `sha256:` form and raw-byte sensitivity are otherwise accurately stated.

R1-7 — MINOR

**Offending diff text:**

> “Core §5 gives a pack a series identity (an absolute-URI `id`) and a `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH` `version`”

**Contradicting source:**

Core §5 specifies the absolute-URI `id` and local-reference scope, but not `version`.

— `spec/judgment-pack-core.md:269`

The three-component version rule is in the §4 root table:

> `version` — “Three-component `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH` revision string”

— `spec/judgment-pack-core.md:247`

**Proposed fix:** Cite “Core §§4–5,” or cite §4 for the fields and §5 for identifier semantics.

R1-8 — MINOR

**Offending diff text:**

> “No two independent implementations have yet emitted and compared this digest (RFC 0001's own Implementation bar)”

**Contradicting source:**

RFC 0001’s bar is broader and different:

> “Two independent implementations should be able to emit and verify a manifest offline and reach the same accept/reject decision on the negative cases.”

— `rfcs/0001-pack-manifest.md:90`

**Proposed fix:** Say, “No two independent implementations have yet met RFC 0001’s manifest emit/verify and negative-case agreement bar.” Retain a narrower digest-comparison claim only if separately sourced.

do not merge

The digest choice itself answers the removed algorithm/canonicalization question, and the runtime form, gateway receipt claim, and Studies 014/016 precedent are supported. The merge is blocked because the amendment turns a still-Draft candidate into an asserted agreement and materially overstates the study evidence; the remaining citation and coherence defects should be corrected in the same revision.

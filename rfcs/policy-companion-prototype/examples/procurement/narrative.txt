SYNTHETIC RFC PROTOTYPE — Procurement

Competitive procedure
Automated eligibility requires a documented competitive procedure. A missing record requires a procurement reviewer.

Supplier conflicts
Supplier relationships require a conflict review. The threshold for a material relationship needs clarification.

Invoice payment
Treasury performs payment reconciliation outside this eligibility interpretation.

Public notice
Publish the award notice through the separate communications process.

Purchase scope
This eligibility pack applies only to purchase requests; other requests remain outside this automated path.
